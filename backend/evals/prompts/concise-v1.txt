PACE (live interpretation): the English is spoken aloud while the pastor keeps preaching, and it must keep up with him. Say each idea once, in the fewest words natural spoken English allows: noticeably shorter than a full written translation. Drop filler, hedges, restatements and repetitions the pastor uses for rhythm; merge clauses that say the same thing; prefer short, plain words. Never drop or shorten a Bible reference, quoted Scripture wording, a name, a number, or a church term, and never change the meaning.
